# Third-party notices

Mnemo has no runtime dependencies. The password strength estimator
(`src/shared/strength/`) contains code ported from, and data generated from,
the works below. The same notices are kept in the shipped files.

## zxcvbn 4.4.2

`src/shared/strength/matching.js`, `scoring.js`, `feedback.js`, and
`data/graphs.js` are ported from zxcvbn (https://github.com/dropbox/zxcvbn).
The MIT license text is in the header of `src/shared/strength/estimator.js`.

Copyright (c) 2012-2016 Dan Wheeler and Dropbox, Inc. MIT License.

## zxcvbn-ts language packages

`src/shared/strength/data/common.js`, `en.js`, `de.js`, and `ru.js` are
generated by `scripts/make-strength-dictionaries.js` from
`@zxcvbn-ts/language-common@4.1.3`, `@zxcvbn-ts/language-en@4.1.1`,
`@zxcvbn-ts/language-de@4.1.1`, and `@zxcvbn-ts/language-ru@4.1.0`
(https://github.com/zxcvbn-ts/zxcvbn), MIT License. The lists are cut to
their most common words; the Russian Wikipedia list also loses markup words
such as "doc" and "https".

### OpenSubtitles 2024 (commonWords)

The `commonWords` lists are derived from OpenSubtitles 2024
(https://opus.nlpl.eu/), provided by Helsinki-NLP / OPUS under the Open Data
Commons Attribution License (ODC-BY 1.0). Original data is attributed to
subtitle contributors and rights holders. The packages contain a processed
derivative: tokenized text, aggregated frequencies, normalization and
filtering.

### EFF large wordlist (diceware)

The `diceware` list is the EFF large wordlist for passphrases
(https://www.eff.org/dice) by the Electronic Frontier Foundation, licensed
under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 United States (CC BY 3.0 US).
