    RECOVERY KEY

    Save your recovery key

    If you forget your master passphrase, this key is the only way back into this vault. Mnemo will not show it again.

    • Write it down or print it, and keep it away from this Mac.
    • Anyone with this key can open your vault.
    • If you saved it as a file, move the file to a safe place.
    • The key only opens this vault’s file or its backups. If the app and its data are deleted, the key alone cannot bring them back — keep an encrypted backup too.

    ENCRYPTED BACKUP

    Save a backup now?

    A backup made now opens with your master passphrase or with the recovery key you just saved. Keep it somewhere other than this Mac, such as an external drive.
