Breached passwords

Checks your passwords against Have I Been Pwned without sending them: only the first 5 characters of each password's SHA-1 hash leave this Mac, and the match is made here. Have I Been Pwned sees your IP address.

Reused passwords

When one site leaks, the same password opens the others. Give every site its own password.

Weak passwords

Short, common, or predictable passwords are guessed quickly. Replace them with generated ones.

        Snapshots on this Mac

        After changes, Mnemo keeps encrypted snapshots of this vault: the last ten, one a day for 30 days, and one before each import, restore, or change of keys. Each opens with the master passphrase that was valid when it was taken.

        Copy outside this Mac

        Snapshots are copied, still encrypted, to a folder you choose in iCloud Drive or on an external disk, and checked after each copy. If this Mac is lost, the vault can be restored from there.

          Recovery key

          Save your recovery key

          If you forget your master passphrase, this key is the only way back into this vault. Mnemo will not show it again.

          Your previous recovery key no longer opens this vault. It still opens backups made before this change.

          • Write it down or print it, and keep it away from this Mac.
          • Anyone with this key can open your vault.
          • If you saved it as a file, move the file to a safe place.
          • The key only opens this vault’s file or its backups. If the app and its data are deleted, the key alone cannot bring them back — keep an encrypted backup too.

          Encrypted backup

          Save a backup now?

          A backup made now opens with your master passphrase or with the recovery key you just saved. Keep it somewhere other than this Mac, such as an external drive.
